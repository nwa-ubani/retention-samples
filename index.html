WHAT FAITH SETS UP

It is designed around how the salon already works.

This is not a one-size-fits-all WhatsApp blast. The flow, timing and words are built around the services, booking method and tone of the business.

01

Map the client journey

Choose services worth following up on and decide when a reminder is genuinely helpful.

02

Connect the information

Use the booking form, spreadsheet, CRM or calendar already in use to capture name, service, date, WhatsApp and email.

03

Build rules and messages

Match care notes, check-ins and return prompts to the actual service. Add a clear route for replies.

04

Test, launch and hand over

Check every path with sample clients, then give the team a simple view of what is running and what needs a human reply.
